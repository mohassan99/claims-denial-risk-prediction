# CPAP/PAP device coding guidelines and diagnosis requirements

## Overview

Positive airway pressure devices fall under the Durable Medical Equipment benefit category. For Medicare payment the equipment must meet the reasonable and necessary standard in the related Local Coverage Determination (L33718) and the specific statutory payment requirements.

## Diagnosis code requirements

The only diagnosis code that supports medical necessity for PAP therapy is G47.33 (obstructive sleep apnea). Having the code does not by itself guarantee coverage: the supplier must verify that all applicable coverage criteria in the LCD are met. ICD-10 codes other than G47.33 are treated as not supporting medical necessity for PAP devices, so other diagnoses cannot justify PAP claims.

## Device coding

E0601: a single-level continuous positive airway pressure device delivers a constant level of positive air pressure through tubing and a noninvasive interface. Auto-titrating single-level CPAP devices also use E0601.

E0470: a bi-level device without backup rate allows independent inspiratory and expiratory pressure settings and assists spontaneous breathing through a nasal, oral or facial mask interface.

E0471: like E0470 but with a timed backup feature that delivers pressure whenever spontaneous inspiratory effort is not sufficient.

## Accessories

A7032 is a replacement nasal mask (unit of service: each). A7033 is a replacement nasal cannula-type interface (unit: pair). A7027 is a combination oral/nasal mask, where one unit of service includes both the oral and the nasal components. A4604 is tubing used with a heated humidifier that has a heated wire running the length of the tubing.

Liners are soft material placed between the patient's skin and the mask interface. They are comfort items with no separate payment and are coded A9270 (noncovered item).

## Monitoring technology

A9279 applies to any type of monitoring technology, whether integrated, modular or standalone. There is no Medicare benefit or payment to DMEPOS suppliers for remote monitoring services.
