# Medicare consultation code elimination, January 1, 2010

## Policy change

Effective January 1, 2010, Medicare stopped recognizing consultation service codes 99241-99245 and 99251-99255 for Part B payment under the physician fee schedule. The change was applied budget-neutrally across inpatient and office/outpatient settings, with limited exceptions for telehealth consultation G-codes.

## Rationale

CMS restructured evaluation and management (E/M) payment. The transmittal states that the agency increased the work relative value units (RVUs) for new and established office visits and increased payment for initial hospital and nursing facility visits. These increased values were also carried into practice expense and malpractice calculations and into the E/M component of global surgical codes.

## What to bill instead, by setting

Hospital inpatient: physicians performing initial evaluations bill initial hospital care codes 99221-99223. More than one physician may bill an initial visit on the same date if each performs an independent evaluation. The principal physician, the one who oversees the patient's care as distinct from other physicians furnishing specialty care, appends modifier AI to the initial visit code. Follow-up visits use subsequent hospital care codes.

Nursing facility: physicians bill initial nursing facility care codes 99304-99306, and the principal physician appends modifier AI. Follow-up visits use codes 99307-99310. Nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists and certified nurse midwives may perform initial visits where state law permits and collaboration and supervision requirements are met.

Office and outpatient: physicians and qualified nonphysician practitioners choose a new or established patient visit code (99201-99215) based on visit complexity and the patient relationship. A new patient is one who has not received professional services from the physician or physician group practice within the previous 3 years.

Critical Access Hospital Method II: appropriate new or established visit codes (99201-99215) are reported on type of bill 85X with revenue codes 96X, 97X or 98X for physicians and nonphysician practitioners with reassigned billing rights.

Rural Health Clinics and Federally Qualified Health Centers: consultation codes were discontinued in favor of office visit codes (99201-99215) and nursing facility codes (99304-99306) as appropriate.

## Documentation

All E/M services must meet existing E/M documentation guidelines. Services should be documented during or immediately after they are provided. Code selection should reflect the actual service content.

## Timeline

The effective date was January 1, 2010, and implementation was January 4, 2010. The transmittal removed all references to consultation codes from Claims Processing Manual Chapter 12, Section 30.6.
